Artist Bio

GoldPlaques is a Sotho Hop music producer blending Northern Sotho cultural sound with modern hip-hop and cinematic production.

Story

Built from raw emotion and street influence, the sound focuses on deep bass, clean drums, and storytelling-driven melodies.
